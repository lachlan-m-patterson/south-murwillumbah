      About the data

      Terrain is a 1 m LiDAR surface model (roofs, trees, bridge deck) and a 1 m bare-earth DEM with river bathymetry. Both are in GDA2020 / MGA zone 56, and the vertical datum is assumed to be AHD. Aerial imagery at 0.25 m comes from NSW Spatial Services. Zoning is from the NSW Planning Portal (Tweed LEP 2014). Roads, rail and parks are from OpenStreetMap contributors. The school lot is from the NSW cadastre. Building boxes were derived from the LiDAR. The levee line was traced from the DEM crest and is indicative only.

      Controls: left-drag pans, right-drag (or two fingers) rotates and tilts, and the scroll wheel zooms. Click a feature or zone for details. Press H to return to the overview, and Esc to stop a tour.
